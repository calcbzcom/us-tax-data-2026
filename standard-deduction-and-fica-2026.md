# 2026 Standard Deduction and FICA Rates (US)

Quick reference table used for paycheck estimates.

## Federal standard deduction, tax year 2026

| Filing status | Standard deduction |
|---|---|
| Single | $16,100 |
| Married filing jointly | $32,200 |
| Head of household | $24,150 |

## FICA payroll tax, 2026

| Item | Rate | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Social Security (employee share) | 6.2% | Wages up to $184,500 |
| Medicare (employee share) | 1.45% | All wages |
| Additional Medicare Tax | 0.9% | Wages above $200,000 |

## Worked example

Gross pay per period: $2,000. Social Security: $2,000 x 6.2% = $124.00. Medicare: $2,000 x 1.45% = $29.00. FICA total: $153.00 per period, before federal and state income tax.
