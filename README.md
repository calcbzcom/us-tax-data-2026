# US Tax Data 2026 - Paycheck Reference

A short reference of the 2026 US federal figures used in the free paycheck calculators at [Calcbz](https://calcbz.com/).

## Federal standard deduction (2026)

| Filing status | Standard deduction |
|---|---|
| Single | $16,100 |
| Married filing jointly | $32,200 |
| Head of household | $24,150 |

## FICA payroll taxes (2026)

- Social Security: 6.2% of wages up to $184,500
- Medicare: 1.45% of all wages
- Additional Medicare: 0.9% on wages above $200,000

## States with no state wage income tax

Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming.

## Gross pay to net pay, step by step

1. Start with gross pay for the pay period.
2. Subtract pre-tax deductions (for example 401(k) and health premiums).
3. Subtract FICA taxes (Social Security and Medicare).
4. Subtract federal income tax withholding (based on Form W-4).
5. Subtract state and local taxes, if any.
6. The result is net (take-home) pay.
